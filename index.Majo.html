Reading: Just Right!

Read the story first. Then scroll down to answer the questions.

Once upon a time, a girl named Goldilocks walked in the forest. She saw a little house and opened the door. Nobody was home.

In the kitchen, there were three bowls of porridge. Papa Bear’s porridge was too hot. Mama Bear’s porridge was too cold. Baby Bear’s porridge was just right, and she ate it all.

Then Goldilocks went to the living room. Papa Bear’s chair was too big. Mama Bear’s chair was too hard. Baby Bear’s chair was just right, but it broke!

Upstairs, she found three beds. Papa Bear’s bed was too hard. Mama Bear’s bed was too soft. Baby Bear’s bed was just right, and Goldilocks fell asleep.

The three bears came home. “Someone ate my porridge!” said Baby Bear. Then they found Goldilocks in the bed. She woke up, screamed, jumped out of the window and ran home. She never went back to the bears’ house.

Reading strategies

Skimming read quickly for the general idea.
Scanning look for one detail, like what Baby Bear’s chair did.
Inference use clues to understand how a character feels.
